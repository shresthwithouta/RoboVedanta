## 🖼️ Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Minimum Size**: 800x800px
- **Recommended**: 1200x1600px (portrait)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 (portrait) or 1:1 (square)
- **File Size**: Under 500KB each (optimize for web)
- **Background**: Professional/plain background preferred
- **Quality**: High resolution, professional headshots

## 📝 Notes

- Images should be professional quality
- Consistent style across all photos preferred
- Photos are displayed on teacher cards and detail pages
- Use tools like TinyPNG or ImageOptim to compress without quality loss

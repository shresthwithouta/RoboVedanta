## 📋 Program Structure (Exact Client Text)

### Step 1: Weekly Hands-On Sessions

> "Each week, students engage in hands-on sessions, allowing them to directly apply their skills and knowledge in robotics."

### Step 2: Project-Based Learning

> "The program emphasizes project-based learning, where students work on real-world challenges to enhance their understanding and creativity."

### Step 3: Structured Lessons

> "Structured lessons provide a clear framework, ensuring that students grasp essential concepts and techniques in robotics."

### Step 4: Regular Assessments

> "Regular assessments help track progress and identify areas for improvement, ensuring continuous learning and development."

### Final Exhibition

> "At the end of the program, students participate in exhibitions to showcase their projects, demonstrating their accomplishments and innovations."

## 🎨 Visual Elements

### Badges

- **Most Popular**: Orange/Accent badge on Simulation course
- **Premium**: Gradient gold badge on Hardware course
- **Inquiry Only**: On school programs card

### Icons

- 📺 Monitor (Simulation course)
- 📦 Package (Hardware course)
- 📅 Calendar (Weekly sessions)
- 🎯 Target (Project-based)
- 📖 BookOpen (Structured lessons)
- 📈 TrendingUp (Assessments)
- 🏆 Award (Final exhibition)
- 🏫 School (School programs)

### Color Coding

- **Golden (#B8860B)**: Pricing, CTAs, accents
- **Deep Blue**: Backgrounds, cards
- **White**: Text, form inputs
- **Shimmer**: Pricing numbers, key headings

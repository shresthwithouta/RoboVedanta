### Option 2: Screen Recording Video

**What to record** (30-60 seconds):

1. **Opening shot**: Hero section with shimmer animation (5s)
2. **Scroll down slowly**: Show seamless blue backgrounds (5s)
3. **Hover over cards**: Demonstrate interactions (10s)
4. **Button hovers**: Show golden effects (5s)
5. **CTA section**: Scale effect on golden card (5s)
6. **Scroll back up**: Show smooth experience (5s)

**Tools**:

- OBS Studio (Free)
- Windows Game Bar (Win + G)
- Loom (Free online tool)
- ShareX (Free with recording)

**Settings**:

- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame rate: 60fps for smooth animations
- Export as MP4

### 1. **Brand Consistency**

"We extracted the exact colors from your logo - the deep blue (#1B4965) and golden accent (#F4C542) - to create a cohesive brand experience."

### 2. **Premium Perception**

"The dark theme with golden accents creates a premium, professional feel that positions RoboVedanta as a high-quality education provider."

### 3. **Unique Animation**

"We created a custom shimmer effect for 'Real Projects' that draws the eye and makes the message memorable - something your competitors don't have."

### 4. **Accessibility**

"High contrast between white text and blue background ensures readability, while the golden accents provide visual interest without overwhelming."

"All transitions use cinematic timing (600-700ms) for a polished, professional feel. Every hover and click feels intentional and premium."
